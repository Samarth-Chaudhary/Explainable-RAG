## 3. Metadata & Domain Schemas

### 3.1 `FeatureDefinition` & `FeatureSchema`
Specifies data types, units, permitted ranges, null handling, derived formulas, and mutability classes:
- **`immutable`**: Cannot change (e.g. `AGE`, `DAYS_EMPLOYED`, `PAST_DELINQUENCIES`, `CREDIT_BUREAU_SCORE`, `AMT_INCOME_TOTAL`).
- **`actionable`**: Actionable levers that may vary in counterfactuals (e.g. `AMT_CREDIT`, `AMT_ANNUITY`, `AMT_GOODS_PRICE`).
- **`derived`**: Automatically recomputed from primitives (e.g. `FOIR_RATIO`, `LTV_RATIO`).
- **`sensitive`**: Protected attributes subject to anti-discrimination and fair lending rules.

### 3.2 `ModelManifest`
Records cryptographic SHA-256 artifact hash, training dataset version, feature schema hash, background explainer reference, and explicit calibration status.

### 3.3 `OntologyRecord`
Provides semantic translation bridge connecting quantitative model features to legal concepts, policy tags, search terms, and retrieval queries.

1. **Primary Verified Ingestion**: UCI "Default of Credit Card Clients" (`dataset_id=350`, 30,000 records, 24 raw attributes, zero missing values, default rate 22.12%).

### 4.2 Field-by-Field Mapping and Transformation Rules

| Schema Feature | Dtype | Unit | Source Column (UCI 350) | Source Column (Home Credit) | Transformation / Normalization Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `AMT_CREDIT` | `float` | `INR` | `LIMIT_BAL` | `AMT_CREDIT` | Principal loan amount requested or credit line sanctioned. Direct real continuous variable. |
| `AMT_ANNUITY` | `float` | `INR` | `PAY_AMT1` (recent repayment installment) | `AMT_ANNUITY` | Proposed monthly loan installment. **Unit conversion**: Home Credit `AMT_ANNUITY` is converted if annual; canonical schema is strictly monthly. UCI `PAY_AMT1` represents monthly repayment. |
| `AMT_GOODS_PRICE` | `float` | `INR` | `LIMIT_BAL * 1.25` (collateral valuation baseline) | `AMT_GOODS_PRICE` | Appraised market collateral value. In Home Credit, missing values (0.09%) imputed using median from train split only. |
| `AMT_INCOME_TOTAL` | `float` | `INR` | Estimated monthly capacity based on `LIMIT_BAL / 3.0 + PAY_AMT1` | `AMT_INCOME_TOTAL / 12.0` | **Unit reconciliation**: Home Credit records gross *annual* income; converted to *monthly* by dividing by 12.0. |
| `EXISTING_DEBT_OBLIGATIONS` | `float` | `INR` | Mean revolving balance across prior cycles: `mean(BILL_AMT2..6)` | Bureau file debt service installments | Monthly existing debt obligations excluding current loan installment. Minimum bound enforced at 0.0. |
| `DAYS_EMPLOYED` | `float` | `days` | Proxy from credit relationship history: `min(AGE - 20, 30) * 365` | `DAYS_EMPLOYED` | Negative integer representing days prior to application. **Sentinel handling**: In Home Credit, `DAYS_EMPLOYED = 365243` represents retired or unemployed status. Handled by mapping `365243 -> 0` and setting a non-negative employment duration. |
| `AGE` | `int` | `years` | `AGE` (range 21–79) | `-DAYS_BIRTH // 365` | Applicant chronological age in completed years. Protected attribute; classified as `mutability="sensitive"`. Strictly frozen in counterfactual search. |
| `CREDIT_BUREAU_SCORE` | `float` | `score` | Calibrated mapping from historical repayment delay statuses (`PAY_0`..`PAY_6`): mapped to 300–900 CIBIL-scale | `EXT_SOURCE_2 * 600.0 + 300.0` | Credit bureau score on canonical 300–900 scale. External credit bureau attributions; immutable in counterfactual generation. |
| `PAST_DELINQUENCIES` | `int` | `count` | Count of delinquency events (`PAY_i >= 2`, i.e., 60+ DPD) in the 6-month repayment track | Count of 30+ DPD bureau delinquency occurrences in last 24 months | Historical adverse events; strictly immutable (`mutability="immutable"`). |

### 4.3 Dynamically Derived Ratios

1. **`FOIR_RATIO` (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio)**:
   $$\text{FOIR\_RATIO} = \frac{\text{AMT\_ANNUITY} + \text{EXISTING\_DEBT\_OBLIGATIONS}}{\text{AMT\_INCOME\_TOTAL}}$$
   - **Range Enforcement**: Clipped to $[0.0, 2.5]$ to prevent division-by-zero anomalies.
   - **Mutability**: `derived_recompute`. When actionable inputs (`AMT_ANNUITY`) change in counterfactual generation, `FOIR_RATIO` is recomputed dynamically via this authoritative formula.

2. **`LTV_RATIO` (Loan to Value Ratio)**:
   $$\text{LTV\_RATIO} = \frac{\text{AMT\_CREDIT}}{\text{AMT\_GOODS\_PRICE}}$$
   - **Range Enforcement**: Clipped to $[0.0, 2.0]$.
   - **Mutability**: `derived_recompute`. Recomputed whenever `AMT_CREDIT` or `AMT_GOODS_PRICE` is adjusted.

### 4.4 Data Split and Hygiene Integrity

- **Stratified Partitioning**: 70% Train (21,000), 15% Validation (4,500), 15% Test (4,500). Fixed seed = 42.
- **Target Distribution**: Constant 22.12% default rate across all three splits.
- **Leakage Prevention**: All imputation statistics (medians, scalers, encoders) are fit strictly on the Train split and applied without refitting to Validation and Test. The Test split is held out and touched exactly once at final evaluation.
